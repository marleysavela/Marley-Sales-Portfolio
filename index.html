Rescuing orphaned accounts

When sales reps leave a company, their accounts can sit with nobody watching them. I found the orphaned accounts in our CRM and matched them with active reps.

What I did

  • Reassigned more than 1,000 accounts from departed employees to active sales representatives.
  • Protected existing pipeline value and helped prevent customer attrition.

What I found

An account with no owner is a customer nobody is talking to. Fixing ownership is quiet work, but it's one of the fastest ways to protect revenue.

Tools: Salesforce

Reports and dashboards for sales leadership

Leaders were tracking pipeline by hand. I built custom Salesforce reports and dashboards to replace those manual methods.

What I did

  • Designed and maintained reports and dashboards that sales leadership adopted.
  • Improved pipeline visibility by 12 percent.
What I learned about dashboards

A dashboard only works if people use it. I got the best results by asking leaders what decision they were trying to make, then building the report around that question instead of around the available fields.

Tools: Salesforce reports and dashboards

Regular CRM data audits

Reliable data is the foundation for every other CRM project. I run recurring audits across the CRM to catch problems before they reach a sales rep.

Record problems I look for in audits
Problem Why it matters Action
Duplicate records Reps can contact the same customer twice, and reports double-count Identify and resolve
Outdated records Reps waste time on stale contact information Update or flag
Incomplete records Missing fields weaken reports and forecasts Fill in or follow up

Along the way I worked with sales, marketing, and leadership to make sure the CRM data matched what the business actually needed.

Tools: Salesforce data administration
